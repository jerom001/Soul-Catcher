# 🎮 Soul Catcher
**Soul Catcher** is a fast-paced arcade game focused on reflex, timing, and survival.  
The player must catch the correct souls while avoiding dangerous ones, with difficulty increasing over time.
This project was built as part of my game development journey, focusing on **core gameplay mechanics**, **player feedback**, and **scalable difficulty systems** rather than heavy polish.

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay Overview
- Catch specific falling souls to score points  
- Avoid harmful souls that reduce lives
- Collect healing souls to restore lost lives
- Limited life system with clear visual and audio feedback
- Difficulty scales dynamically as the score increases  
- Simple controls with increasing pressure over time  

The addition of healing souls introduces a light risk vs reward layer — players must decide whether to prioritize safety or chase recovery opportunities during high-pressure moments.

The experience is designed to be easy to understand, yet increasingly intense over time.

---

## 🧠 Core Concepts
- Centralized GameManager architecture
- Event driven collision handling
- Score based dynamic difficulty scaling
- Life management system with healing mechanic
- Game state transition (Start → Play → Game Over → Restart)
- Feedback systems (camera shake, particles, SFX)
